Much like our cities, our minds are built on failed attempts, failed predictions of how we will manage to succeed. When each begins to crumble, we could never afford the cost of tearing it all down and rebuilding from scratch. There has to be some sense of good enough, some degree to which we're willing to live with the building, even if it leaks now and again. We patch, adapt, and every now and again, try a renovation.

For our minds, these attempts at building something that will meet our needs are fraught with missteps and limitations. I want to exercise as my doctor suggests, but still manage to make choices that render this inconcievable in the moment. How did I get here?

Much like the cost of starting the building project all over again, our minds are seeking low energy ways to manage. Can we get by? We surely don't need to pay all that money for an electrician right now, do we? The expectations our bodies and minds generate can feel sticky. Yes, I really want to feel connected to my wife, but I don't really need to apologize, do I? Maybe I just continue and hope that it's fine for both of us.

This desire to minimize cost, to minimize energy, allows us to function under less than ideal circumstances and to even enjoy the familiarity of our apartment, even that leak we never fixed. We might even take pride in it as we have decided to live more frugally.

To this end, we find ways to enjoy the process of saving, of getting by on less. It's more efficient, but it does come with costs. We might even come up with fantasies around why this is preferable to the alternatives.

Just as we save money on a building project to get by and then create fantasies to allow us to feel more comfortable and enjoy our experience living and working there, our mind may generate affects (i.e. the things that you feel) that through our learning guides the development of important ideas and fantasies that allow us to live in our space.

Most often, this works really well. Yes, we might not live in a mansion, but it's liveable, you know?

But at times, we find ourselves stuck. My snacking habit continues on and frustrates me, but I continue it. I avoid the exercise I know I ought to do, perhaps even making ridiculous choices along the way, like staying up later the previous night. These choices often feel below our awareness, below our level of conscious choices, because they have become so practiced that we don't even need to notice them any more.

The frustration builds, the consequences mount, and the beat goes on.

The failure that now frustrates us is a merely our energy savings writ large. And our fantasies default to past, even when those were the very things that first justified the presence of the beliefs, emotions, and actions.

The identities that made so much sense now are frozen in place. The thing we need so badly to fix everything consistently appears to recede just out of reach. We display our own instability to others, hoping that they will fix us or at least confirm for us that we're crazy.

As the failure intensifies, we find ourselves doubling, tripling, quadrupling down on the very things that made life seem intelligible, manageable.

Our mind is sticky and defaults to what it knows. Change requires frustrating the beliefs, emotions, and action tendenciecs maintaining the energy of the system, enough so that we can bring these into our awareness, our working memory, and try to interrupt them.
